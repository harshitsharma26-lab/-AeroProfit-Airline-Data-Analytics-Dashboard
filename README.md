# ✈️ AeroProfit — Airline Data Analytics & Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

**AeroProfit** is a data analytics and business intelligence project focused on analyzing airline performance, profitability, passenger activity, routes, and operational trends.

The project transforms raw airline data into meaningful business insights using **SQL, Python/Jupyter Notebook, and Power BI**.

The main objective of this project is to understand airline business performance and identify trends that can help stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering key business questions such as:

* How is airline revenue and profit changing over time?
* Which airlines/routes generate the highest revenue?
* What are the passenger and booking trends?
* Which destinations or routes perform best?
* What factors contribute to airline profitability?
* How does operational performance vary across airlines?
* Which areas may require business improvement?
